Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

1. What types of wine do you sell?

We offer a curated selection of red, white, rosé, and sparkling wines from top vineyards.

2. Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within our country only. We’re working on expanding our shipping regions!

3. How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you’ll receive a tracking link via email.

4. Can I return or exchange my wine?

Due to alcohol laws, we cannot accept returns on wine unless there is an issue with the product. Please contact us if you have any concerns.

5. How do I sign up for the newsletter?

Use the newsletter signup form in our footer to get exclusive offers and updates!

6. Who do I contact for help?

Email us anytime at support@roseswineshop.com.
